What is AI News Detector?

AI News Detector is a web-based application designed to analyze news articles and determine their credibility using AI-powered language processing. It evaluates patterns, sources, and content structures to assess authenticity.

How Does It Work?

Using a trained AI model, the system scans the inputted news link, processes the text, and delivers an analysis regarding its reliability based on various factors such as writing style, source legitimacy, and detected biases.
